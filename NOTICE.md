# Third-party components

This repository contains only scripts. Models and libraries are downloaded separately and keep their own licences.

- **pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1** — speaker diarization pipeline by pyannote, distributed under CC BY 4.0. Access requires accepting conditions on Hugging Face.
- **pyannote.audio** — diarization toolkit, MIT licence.
- **whisper.cpp** — C/C++ port of OpenAI Whisper by Georgi Gerganov and contributors.
- **OpenAI Whisper model weights** (`ggml-medium.bin`) — published by OpenAI.
- **PyTorch**, **FFmpeg**, **soundfile**, **huggingface_hub** — see their projects for licence terms.

Check each licence before redistributing models or binaries.
